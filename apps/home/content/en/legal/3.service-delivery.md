# Service Delivery Policy

This policy describes how ecoma.io delivers the services you subscribe to:
where they run, how they are maintained, and how operational incidents are
handled.

## Delivery model

The service is delivered as a hosted platform served from regional edge
infrastructure. You do not need to install or maintain server software to use
it.

The public website and documentation are served under a locale prefix, so every
URL names the language it renders in.

## Maintenance and changes

Routine maintenance is performed without service interruption wherever the
platform allows it. When a maintenance window cannot be avoided, it is
scheduled and announced in advance.

## Incidents and status

Operational incidents are triaged as they are detected. We work to restore
service first, then to publish an explanation of the cause and the fix for
significant incidents.

## Support

Support is provided through the channels published on the service surface you
use. Response expectations depend on your subscription tier and the severity of
the issue.
