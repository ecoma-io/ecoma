# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data ecoma.io collects when you use its
public website and services, why that data is processed, and the choices
available to you.

We collect only the data that is needed to operate, secure and improve the
service, and we do not sell personal data.

## Data we collect

Account data: identifiers you provide when you register or contact us, such as
your name, email address and organisation.

Usage data: technical records generated by your interaction with the site, such
as requested pages, response status and coarse device characteristics. This
data is used in aggregate form for capacity and reliability work.

## How data is used

To deliver the service you requested, to authenticate you, to prevent abuse,
and to communicate service-related announcements.

To measure aggregate usage so we can understand which surfaces are useful and
keep them reliable.

## Sharing and processing

Personal data is processed by ecoma.io and, where strictly necessary, by
service providers acting on our behalf under contract. Each provider is limited
to the data required for its function.

We may disclose data when required by applicable law, and only to the extent
required.

## Retention and your rights

Personal data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above,
after which it is deleted or de-identified.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by
contacting us. We respond to verified requests within the timeframe required by
applicable law.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially, we publish the updated version on this
page and, where appropriate, announce the change through the service before it
takes effect.
