# Ecoma Doctrine

The design ecoma commits to, published as it is settled rather than after the
fact: the North Stars, the specifications each mechanism must satisfy, the
charters, and the rubric the reviews run against.

These pages are **design end-state, pre-implementation**. They describe what the
system is meant to be and why each decision was taken, including the ones that
were reversed. They are not a description of shipped software.

The documents live in `shared/libs/doctrine` and are licensed
