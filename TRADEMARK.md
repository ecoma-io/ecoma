# Ecoma Trademark Policy

"Ecoma" and the Ecoma logo are trademarks of Ecoma. This policy says what you
may do with them. It is separate from the source-code licence in
[`LICENSE`](./LICENSE), and the two do different jobs: the licence governs the
code, this policy governs the name.

That separation is the point. The code is source-available precisely so it can
be read, self-hosted and modified — and a licence that permits modification
without a trademark policy leaves no way to tell a modified build apart from
ours. Users who cannot make that distinction cannot hold anyone accountable
for what a build does with their data, which is the one thing the whole
project is built to make legible.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your software is.** "Works with Ecoma", "built on Ecoma",
  "an Ecoma driver", "compatible with Ecoma" — nominative use that describes a
  true relationship is fine, as long as it does not suggest we produced,
  endorsed or support your product.
- **Say that you run it.** "We self-host Ecoma", "our workflows run on Ecoma".
- **Write about it.** Articles, talks, tutorials, courses, comparisons and
  reviews may use the name freely, including commercially. Screenshots and the
  logo may be used to illustrate them.
- **Reproduce the notices.** Redistributing the software under `LICENSE`
  requires you to keep our notices intact, so keeping the name where it
  already appears is not only permitted, it is required.

## What needs our written permission

- **Naming your own product, service or company** Ecoma, or a name close
  enough to be confused with it — including domain names, social accounts and
  package names in any registry.
- **Distributing a modified build under the name Ecoma.** Modify all you like;
  the licence grants you that. But a modified build must not present itself as
  Ecoma, because then the name no longer identifies who is answerable for it.
  Call it something of your own and describe the relationship instead — "based
  on Ecoma" is accurate and permitted.
- **Offering a hosted or managed Ecoma service.** Note that the licence
  already forbids this conduct regardless of what you call it; the trademark
  is a second, independent reason.
- **Using the logo as your own**, or altering it.
